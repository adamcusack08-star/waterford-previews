About us

Irish-owned, and local since 1986

J.E.C Security Ltd. was founded in 1986 by Joseph E. Crowley and has grown into one of the largest privately owned electronic security companies in the country.

We employ 16 installation staff, 6 service staff and 4 administration staff, all trained by us on modern technology. We have all our own equipment, including scaffolding, hoists and mobile towers, and we're fully licensed and insured.

Private ownership means you'll always deal with J.E.C Security, not a faceless organisation that keeps changing hands, and with local staff committed to excellent personal service.
