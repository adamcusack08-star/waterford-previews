About us

Meet Michael Guiry

Michael Guiry has over 20 years' experience in the property market. Coming from a farming background, he understands all aspects of selling or buying land.

Michael qualified in 2002 from Galway-Mayo Institute of Technology and opened Guiry Auctioneers in 2007. In 2021 the business became Guiry Real Estate.

We help people buy or sell their homes, land and holiday homes. We operate as traditional auctioneers, and we pride ourselves on our customer service: you'll be kept informed at every stage.
