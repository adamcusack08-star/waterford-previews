About us

A Waterford family business since 1979

Byrne Gas was established in 1979 by Pat and Paula Byrne and has been serving the people of Waterford city and county ever since.

We install new central heating systems, convert old ones, replace boilers, and fit renewable energy systems and heat pumps. We also sell and install gas appliances, and offer an unbeatable after-sales service.

All our staff are highly skilled, insured and qualified to National Standards Authority of Ireland requirements, and every system is installed to IS813 and RGII approved standards. Because quality, safety and customer care matter so much, every installation is carried out by Byrne Gas's own people.
