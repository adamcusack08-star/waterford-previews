About us

Established in New Ross in 2010

B&R TranSpares Limited supplies OE quality and aftermarket alternative parts for commercial vehicles. Our warehouse is fully stocked with parts for all makes of truck, bus and trailer.

Our sales team has over 40 years of experience, and we import some of the world's leading brands from across Europe, so we can stay competitive on OE quality parts.
